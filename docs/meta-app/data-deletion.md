# Data Deletion Instructions

**Effective date:** 16 October 2026  
**Last updated:** 16 October 2026

You can ask us to delete personal information processed through Spark Agent.

## How to request deletion

Email admin@growsparkconsulting.com with the subject "Data deletion request". Include:

- The phone number used in the WhatsApp conversation, including country code.
- The business name or WhatsApp number you contacted, if known.
- Approximate dates of the conversation.
- Whether you want a particular conversation deleted or all information associated with you in that business's Service account.
- A safe way to contact you if we need to verify the request.

Do not email identity documents or sensitive information unless we specifically request the minimum needed to verify your identity through a secure method.

You may also reply "STOP" in WhatsApp to stop further automated messages. STOP opts you out of messaging; it does not by itself delete existing conversation records. To request deletion, email us using the instructions above. You may also contact the business directly, since it controls its customer conversations.

## What happens next

We will acknowledge the request and process it in accordance with applicable law. We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you are the person concerned or are authorized to act for them. We will locate the relevant business and records, coordinate with that business where needed, and delete or de-identify the information we control when the request is valid, subject to applicable law and the business's instructions.

Customer chats and messages are retained until a business or contact submits a deletion request. Chats for an eligible unpaid trial tenant are deleted as part of tenant cleanup 30 days after the trial ends. Security and diagnostic logs are retained for up to 30 days in our hosting infrastructure logs. Backups are maintained according to our database-provider plan (currently 7 days for automated daily database backups on Supabase Pro, with rolling weekly disaster-recovery dumps retained for up to 4 weeks). Database backups do not include uploaded Storage media. Billing/tax records are retained for as long as required by applicable Indian accounting and tax laws, and deleted when no longer required.

If a business controls the relevant records, we will forward or coordinate the request and tell you how to contact that business. We may retain limited information where required by law or needed for security, fraud prevention, billing, legal claims, or compliance. Backup copies are used for recovery and are not used for ordinary access.

We will confirm completion or explain any legal or operational reason we cannot complete all or part of the request. Contact admin@growsparkconsulting.com if you have questions or want to raise a privacy grievance.
