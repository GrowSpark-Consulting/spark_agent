# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 16 October 2026  
**Last updated:** 16 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Grow Spark Consulting, operating the Spark Agent service ("we", "us"), handles personal information when you visit sparkagent.in, contact us, or interact with a business that uses our WhatsApp automation and inbox service (the "Service"). The Service uses Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform to route messages between customers and businesses.

## Who is responsible for your information

When a real-estate or other business uses the Service to answer its customers, that business decides why it collects customer information and what it asks the Service to do. The business is responsible for its customer-facing notices and instructions. We process those customer messages on the business's behalf to provide and secure the Service. Contact the business directly for questions about a particular conversation or to exercise a request concerning that conversation; you may also contact us at admin@growsparkconsulting.com and we will route or assist with the request.

For information we collect about our own customers, prospective customers, website visitors, and business contacts, Grow Spark Consulting determines the purposes and means of processing and is responsible for that processing.

## Information we handle

Depending on how the Service is used, information may include:

- WhatsApp profile name, phone number, message content, attachments or voice notes that the business enables the Service to process, message timestamps, and delivery or interaction status.
- Details a person chooses to share, such as property requirements, budget, location, preferred visit time, and language.
- Conversation summaries, AI-generated replies, handoff requests, and staff notes or actions in the business inbox.
- Business account and configuration information, connected WhatsApp Business Account and phone-number identifiers, user and access records, and service usage, credit, billing, and support information.
- Website and service operation data such as IP address, browser/device information, security events, and basic diagnostic logs.

Please do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your enquiry. Businesses should not use the Service to process information they are not authorized to provide to us.

## How we use information

We use information to:

- Receive and display customer messages for the relevant business and generate or deliver replies requested by that business.
- Summarize enquiries, support human handoff, arrange follow-up or visit requests, and operate the business inbox.
- Authenticate users, connect and maintain WhatsApp integrations, prevent abuse, enforce message limits, troubleshoot errors, and protect the Service.
- Provide customer support, administer subscriptions and credits, process payments where applicable, and maintain business records.
- Meet legal obligations and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

AI-generated responses can be inaccurate or incomplete. A business remains responsible for deciding whether to rely on or send a response and should provide human review where appropriate.

## WhatsApp and opt-out

Messages are transmitted through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform and are also subject to WhatsApp/Meta terms and privacy information. A person can ask the business to stop automated messages by replying "STOP" or an equivalent opt-out instruction supported by the business. The Service records the opt-out as needed to honor it. A person may also block or report a WhatsApp account through WhatsApp.

## When we share information

We disclose information only as needed to provide or protect the Service, as instructed by the relevant business, or as required by law. Recipients may include:

- The business operating the WhatsApp number and its authorized staff, through that business's inbox and connected tools.
- Meta / WhatsApp, which carries the messages and provides the WhatsApp Business Platform.
- Providers used to operate the Service: Railway (API hosting and runtime logs), Vercel (website and dashboard hosting), Supabase (database, authentication and file storage), Inngest (background jobs), Anthropic (AI models), MongoDB Atlas Model API using Voyage embeddings, Langfuse (optional AI tracing), Sentry (optional error monitoring), PayU (payments), Google (optional Calendar integration), and Resend (authentication email delivery). The repository specifies Mumbai as the intended Supabase region and the EU Langfuse Cloud endpoint as the default tracing destination. Railway is planned for a region close to Mumbai. It does not specify processing regions for the other providers, and live account settings may differ.
- Professional advisers, authorities, or other parties when legally required or necessary to protect rights, safety, and the Service.

When enabled, Anthropic processes message content and relevant business-provided context to generate requested AI outputs. The embeddings provider processes knowledge-base text and search queries to create embeddings. PayU processes transaction information needed for payment. Google receives calendar information when a business connects calendar features. Meta/WhatsApp carries messages between the customer and business.

Langfuse tracing is off unless configured with its credentials. When enabled, traces include operational details such as model, token and cost data, tenant and conversation identifiers, prompt labels, timing and error codes. Message text is not included by default. If `LANGFUSE_CAPTURE_TEXT` is set to `true`, input and reply text is sent after phone numbers and email addresses are masked and the text is shortened. The repository does not establish the current setting in any live environment. Sentry receives selected redacted error details when configured; it is not sent request bodies or customer message text by the reporting code.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use customer conversation content to train a general-purpose AI model unless the business and affected individuals are given appropriate notice and a lawful basis, and the practice is separately approved and disclosed.

## International processing

The repository specifies Mumbai, India as the intended database region. The default Langfuse Cloud endpoint is in the EU. Other providers may process information in other locations; their precise processing locations depend on their services and account settings. Where information is transferred across borders, we apply the safeguards required by applicable law and our provider agreements.

## Retention

Customer chats and messages are retained until the customer or business requests deletion, or until the account is closed. Eligible unpaid trial business accounts and their tenant data are deleted 30 days after the trial ends; this is a separate account-deletion process.

Security and diagnostic logs are retained for up to 30 days in our hosting infrastructure logs. Backups are retained according to our database provider's backup plan; current period is confirmed by our infrastructure team. Billing/tax records are retained for as long as required by applicable Indian accounting and tax laws, and deleted when no longer required.

## Security

We use administrative and technical safeguards intended to protect information, including access controls and protection of integration credentials. No online service can guarantee absolute security. If a security incident affects personal information, we will take steps required by applicable law and our agreements.

We may need to verify the request and coordinate with the business that controls the conversation.
