# 14 Oct (Dev 1): the daily health jobs for Embedded Signup and assisted connections, and the coexistence rule (PR 4). What was built, and what touches Dev 2's and Dev 3's areas

Dev 1 files only: `backend/src/channels/whatsapp/connect/` (`health.ts`, `checks.ts`, `run-checks.ts`, `recheck.ts`, `embedded-signup.ts`, and their tests; `token-expiry.ts` is unchanged and follows the constant) and the contract doc. No shared file, no SQL, nothing of Dev 2's or Dev 3's changed. Nothing ran against a real Meta account: UNVERIFIED.

## 1. The two jobs now cover three methods

1. `HEALTH_METHODS` (`health.ts`) is `["manual_byo", "embedded_signup", "assisted"]`. The daily sweep (`dueConnections`), the per-connection guard (`checkConnectionHealth`) and the 7-day expiry warning (`token-expiry.ts`, which imports the constant) all follow it. `platform` is not in it, and `failed`, `disconnected`, `validating` and `pending` rows are still skipped by the same `status = 'active'` filters (tested per method).
2. **Unchanged, exactly**: the compare-and-set on `status` and `updated_at`, the failure rule (Meta's word that a token is finished fails the number at once; anything else only on a repeat at least 12 hours later), what is audited, the alert (`notify.send`, `staff_alert`, from the business's own active connection), the 20-hour re-check gap, the 500-per-sweep cap.
3. **What differs between the methods**: (a) no `app_secret_enc`: the job only reads `token_enc`, with the same decrypt context, so nothing changes. (b) The webhook check for `embedded_signup` and `assisted` passes when an app is subscribed to the account (our own app signs their webhooks) and never waits for a signed webhook; for all methods the daily job never posts to `subscribed_apps` (none subscribed is a `warn`, never a failure). (c) The token's expiry comes from `debug_token` as before (a date is stored, 0 clears it, nothing leaves it); whether Meta's business tokens expire at all is UNVERIFIED. PR 2 and PR 3 already store it at connect time.

## 2. Coexistence numbers

A coexistence number also runs in the WhatsApp Business app. What Meta labels it with in `platform_type` is not known (UNVERIFIED), and the `number_registered` check used to fail on any label other than `CLOUD_API`, which would have set a healthy coexistence number `failed` at connect time or, two days running, in the daily job.

1. `checkNumber` takes `coexistence`. For a coexistence number the `platform_type` test can only **pass or be `not_verified`**, never `fail` (the `not_verified` words: "This number also runs in the WhatsApp Business app, so we can't confirm its Cloud API setup from here. It is in the right account."). `overall` is then `warn` and the connection stays `active`.
2. The in-account test still applies (a coexistence number that is not in the account fails). A number that is **not** coexistence still fails on an odd `platform_type`, for every method (tested for the three methods, connect, recheck and health).
3. It is applied in one place, `runGraphChecks`, and passed from the three callers: the Embedded Signup and assisted connect (`input.coexistence`), a recheck and the daily job (the stored `coexistence` column; a row with no value counts as not coexistence). The manual connect form has no coexistence concept and is unchanged.

For Dev 3: a coexistence connection can now show `number_registered: not_verified` with the words above and `overall: warn`; the shapes are unchanged. For Dev 2: nothing.

## 3. Known limits, and what is not in this PR

1. **Wording.** The alert text for a failed connection is the failing check's own message, and the expiry warning says "Create a new one in Meta and connect the number again before then". Both are written for a manual connection. For a number connected with Facebook the right advice is to connect again with Facebook (and "Check the Account ID and the Phone number ID", "your Meta app" are wrong for it too). Not changed here, as agreed; a small separate item (method-aware wording for the token-expired, token-failed, not-in-account and webhook messages and the expiry warning).
2. Alerts still reach only a business with a second active number until Dev 2's fallback to the platform number exists (see the health-jobs task note).
3. **The daily job can turn a connect-time webhook warning into a pass.** For a method other than `manual_byo` the webhook check passes when *any* app is subscribed to the account. PR 2 saves `warn` ("we couldn't subscribe your app") when our own subscription failed but another app (a BSP the number is leaving) is listed; the next daily check, which never subscribes, writes `pass` ("Spark Agent's app is subscribed"), which may be false. A recheck by the owner already did the same. Not changed here (the failure rule and the checks are to stay as they are): the real fix is to compare the listed app with ours, and the shape of Meta's `subscribed_apps` answer is not known (UNVERIFIED). To do with a live account.
4. **`coexistence` is the browser's word** (PR 2's known limit, now also relevant here): a wrong `true` for a normal number skips `register` and now also stops the `platform_type` test from failing it, so it is saved active with `overall: warn` and the daily job never fails it. Before this PR the check would have caught it. Worth reading the number's own `platform_type` before trusting the flag, once a real account shows what a coexistence number reports.
5. Whether an `embedded_signup` or `assisted` token expires, and what Meta labels a coexistence number with, need a real account.
